# Anicca — Playbook: Learning From X

X is where practitioners post the concrete details: the tool that
worked, the price that converted, the posting rhythm, the funnel step
that moved. Anicca treats X as a primary research surface. Findings
are not collected for reading — every scout run ends with at least
one find implemented, or it did not happen.

## Evidence discipline

- Numbers in posts are self-reported until proven otherwise. Every
  case in our notes is labelled: proof link attached (for example a
  public payment profile), or unconfirmed.
- A claim with no source link is not a finding. Every entry carries
  the post URL it came from.
- Inflated presentation is never copied. When a headline number and
  the body of the same post disagree, we copy the mechanism and
  ignore the headline.

## Patterns that keep recurring

Across dozens of earning accounts, the same shapes repeat:

- Story and reaction hooks outperform feature announcements.
- Save-worthy slideshow and list formats travel across platforms.
- A comment keyword that triggers a useful reply or message turns
  readers into a list.
- One long-lived asset, re-shared with a fresh hook, beats a stream
  of one-off posts.
- Search surfaces compound: app-store search, video search, image
  boards, and question pages keep paying after posting stops.
- Pricing differs by channel, and the channel's winner sets the
  anchor — never our guess.

## Copy, don't admire

1. Open the actual post. Read the whole thread, not the screenshot.
2. Name the mechanism in one sentence: hook, format, cadence, funnel.
3. Implement it the same day, in our own product's words.
4. Measure it in the same columns as everything else (see
   PLAYBOOK-MEASUREMENT.md) and keep or kill it by conversion, not
   by views.

## Hook bank

Hooks are kept as a labelled bank per product — affirmation app,
agent products — each hook tagged with the source post it was
adapted from. A hook with no source is an invention, and inventions
are not posted. The bank grows from scouting, never from brainstorming
in a closed room.

## Operating rules for our own X accounts

- Posting is done from the browser, by hand, on the account itself.
  Third-party schedulers are never used for X.
- One account copies one fixed winner's form (see WINNERS.md);
  build-in-public posts carry real numbers from the public
  dashboard, https://aniccaai.com/open/ .
- Every post is reported as a pair: our post URL and the winner
  original URL it copies.
- Scout passes run in both Japanese and English; a pattern proven in
  one language is re-tested, not assumed, in the other.

## What we never copy

- Numbers we cannot verify, presented as if they were ours.
- Engagement bait that breaks a platform's rules.
- Any format that depends on a specific person's face, story, or
  credentials we do not have. If it cannot be reproduced exactly,
  it is not a winner for us — however large the account is.
