# Anicca — Playbook: Measurement

Nothing counts unless it is measured, and a measurement counts only if
it is saved. Platform dashboards change and disappear; the permanent
ledger is the asset. Every number Anicca reports — in public, at
https://aniccaai.com/open/ — comes from a first-hand reading, never
from an estimate.

## One entry per post

Every published post gets one ledger entry holding, together:

- The post URL, the winner original URL, and the winner's account —
  a post that cannot name its source is not a copy and is not counted.
- The format, the length or slide count, the hook, and the caption type.
- Snapshots of the numbers at fixed times (below). A field that could
  not be taken is written as "unmeasured" — never left blank, and never
  confused with a measured zero.

## Fixed snapshot times

- +1 hour: views, likes, comments only. A post still at zero after one
  hour is flagged as an early anomaly and fixed first, by hand.
- +24 hours and +7 days: the full set — views, completion rate, likes,
  comments, shares, saves, profile visits, link taps.
- +30 days: views and conversions only, to catch the long tail.

Fixed times matter because they separate a slow start from a dead post,
and because platform analytics windows close: TikTok data older than
about 60 days is incomplete, so exporting to our own ledger is not
optional (Reelytics' TikTok series practice:
https://www.reelytics.io/blog/tiktok-series-analytics-measure-episodes-retention-revenue ;
ClipSpeed's 48-hour / 7-day columns:
https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/analytics-track-clip-performance.html ).

## Rank by rates, not by views

Weekly reviews rank posts by rates, not raw views:

- Completion rate (healthy ranges by length, per Shortzly:
  https://shortzly.com/blog/short-form-video-analytics-growth-metrics )
- Shares per 1,000 views (2–3 per 1,000 is strong, per HypeNest:
  https://hypenest.ai/blogs/ai-video-repurposing-complete-guide )
- Saves rate, profile-visit rate, and the rate that matters most:
  installs and new customers per post.

The winner's posts are measured in the same columns. The gap between
our rates and the winner's rates is the work list.

## The money pipeline

Posts are the top of one pipeline, and it is read end to end:
post → engagement → profile visit → link tap → install → new
customer → revenue. Costs are always read next to revenue, and the
judge is gross profit — revenue minus cost — never revenue alone.

## One public dashboard

There is exactly one dashboard. It is updated every 30 minutes, in US
dollars only, with per-platform revenue for this month, last month,
and all time, plus app installs and aggregate social metrics. Free
text is limited to a one-line state; the dashboard speaks in numbers.

## "Unmeasured" is a failure, not a footnote

If a number is missing, the job is to go and get it from the primary
source — the platform screen, the billing email, the store console —
in the same run. Writing "unmeasured" and moving on is how companies
stop being believed. And before anything is called done, the final
artifact itself is opened and read in full: file sizes, commit hashes,
and keyword matches are not verification.
