# Anicca — Playbook: Distribution Priorities

How Anicca decides where distribution effort goes. Distribution itself
makes no money — the product behind it does — so distribution is kept
free and organic, and every hour of it is ranked against the revenue it
is expected to produce. The live numbers are public at
https://aniccaai.com/open/ .

## Two layers, both mandatory

- Outside distribution: social posts, articles, search, and discovery
  that bring people to the product.
- Inside-platform distribution: the platform's own search, titles,
  keywords, categories, thumbnails, and ranking, which decide whether a
  published item is ever found. A listing nobody finds is a distribution
  failure, not a finished job.

## Rank by expected revenue per unit of effort

Every channel and product pair is ranked by one measure: expected
revenue divided by the effort it takes to run. Effort means production
time, posting time, and maintenance — not vanity reach.

- Channels that convert (a view becomes an install, a click becomes a
  sale) outrank channels that only accumulate views.
- The ranking is recomputed from measured numbers, never from memory or
  impressions: posts, engagement, installs, new customers, revenue,
  cost, gross profit.
- When the ranking changes, effort moves the same day. Low-efficiency
  lanes are cut; high-efficiency lanes get more volume.
- Volume follows the fixed winners: copy the winner in that space, then
  do more quantity than the winner (see PLAYBOOK-WINNER-COPY.md and
  POSTING-SCHEDULE.md).

## What we do not do

- No paid advertising. Organic distribution is the discipline; paying
  for reach hides whether the copy actually works.
- No reposting other people's work as our own. Winners are copied as
  formats and strategies, with the source named — never passed off as
  original.
- No account farming. One account has one owner, one fixed winner, and
  one main product. Consistency is the brand.
- No drafts as results. A post counts only at a public URL; a listing
  counts only when it is live and findable inside its platform.

## Verify before scaling

Before any lane gets more volume, three things are checked first-hand:

- The money path works end to end: the product page, the payment link,
  and the payout route, with no human step hidden inside it.
- The platform's rules and limits for the account are known, so volume
  does not trip a block that kills the lane.
- The winner being copied is verified: the actual winning post opened,
  its numbers read, its format reproducible.

## The loop

Rank the lanes. Fill the fixed slots. Measure everything. Re-rank.
Repeat daily — quantity, quality, and copy depth all above yesterday.
