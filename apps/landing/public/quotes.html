30 lines for the moment the spiral starts

Free. No sign-up to read. Save the ones that land.

Late nights

  • The story your mind is telling you at this hour is not the truth. Sleep is allowed to be the answer.
  • Nothing has to be solved before morning.
  • You can put the phone down and still be a good person.
  • Tired thoughts are not wise thoughts. Let them wait.
  • Tonight you only need to breathe out slower than you breathe in.

Anxiety

  • Anicca means impermanence. The feeling you are in right now is also passing.
  • A racing heart is a body trying to protect you, not proof that something is wrong.
  • You do not have to believe every "what if."
  • Name five things you can see. You are here, not in the future.
  • You have survived every hard minute so far.

Self-doubt

  • The version of you from a year ago would be proud you are still here.
  • Being unsure is not the same as being wrong.
  • You are allowed to be a beginner in public.
  • Speak to yourself the way you would speak to a friend who is trying.
  • One small step counts, even if nobody sees it.

Burnout

  • Rest is not the reward for finishing. It is the requirement for continuing.
  • Your worth is not measured in output.
  • Doing less today is a decision, not a failure.
  • The work will still be there. So will you, if you rest.
  • You can be committed and still close the laptop.

Grief

  • A storm is not a sign that you built the house wrong.
  • Grief is love with nowhere to go. Let it move.
  • There is no schedule for missing someone.
  • Some days, getting up is the whole achievement.
  • You can carry the loss and still let light in.

Boundaries

  • You are allowed to disappoint people who never asked how you were.
  • "No" is a complete sentence.
  • Protecting your energy is not selfish.
  • You do not owe anyone instant replies.
  • The people who matter will respect the line you draw.
