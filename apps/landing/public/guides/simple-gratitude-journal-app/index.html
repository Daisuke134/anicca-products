A simple gratitude journal app: three entries a day

Why the three-good-things format works, and how to keep a gratitude journal on iPhone with a streak.

What is the three good things format?

Each day you write three things you are grateful for. It is short enough to keep doing.

How does Thankful help?

Thankful is an iPhone gratitude journal: write three entries a day, read daily affirmations and track your journaling streak.

What if I miss a day?

Start again the next day. The streak is a nudge, not a test.
