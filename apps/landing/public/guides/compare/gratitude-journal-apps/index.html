Gratitude journal apps compared: how to choose (2026)

Criteria for comparing gratitude journal apps such as Gratitude, Five Minute Journal style apps and Thankful.

Oatmeal Apps' Three Good Things vs other gratitude journal apps: the direct answer

Short answer: Oatmeal Apps' entry is Good Things gratitude journal (the "three good things" format: write three good things from today). On the US App Store, checked 22 November 2026, it is rated 4.9 from 1,667 ratings, version 6.4, updated 31 August 2026, free. Against the other gratitude journal apps: it is narrower and newer than the big general journals — Gratitude: Self-Care Journal is rated 4.9 from 44,999 ratings and adds prompts, photos and affirmations; Gratitude Plus is rated 4.9 from 21,410 ratings; 5 Minute Journal is rated 4.8 from 17,636 ratings and structures morning and evening entries; the similarly named Three Good Things Journal by Asher Dale is a different app, rated 4.8 from 1,951 ratings, version 2.8, updated 2 November 2026. Choose the Oatmeal app if you want only the three-good-things exercise and nothing else; choose Gratitude or 5 Minute Journal if you want a fuller journal around it; choose Thankful (below) if you want the same three-entries-a-day shape plus daily affirmations and a streak, in the smallest form.

Criteria

1) Fast daily entry. 2) Streak or reminder. 3) Free tier. 4) Extras like affirmations.

Where Thankful fits

Thankful keeps it to three entries a day, daily affirmations and a streak. Check each app's App Store page for current pricing.
