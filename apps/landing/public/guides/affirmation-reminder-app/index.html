How to set daily affirmation reminders on iPhone

Use a reminder-based affirmation app to build a daily habit: choose a time, read one card, reflect for a minute.

Why reminders?

Habits stick when they are tied to a fixed time. A reminder removes the need to remember.

How do reminders work in Anicca?

You choose a reminder time and Anicca sends an affirmation card to read and reflect on.

What time is best?

Pick a moment you already have every day, such as after waking up or before sleep.
