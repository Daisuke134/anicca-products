Is there an app that explains what a text message means?

Use AI to explore possible interpretations of a confusing message and draft a reply, without treating it as fact.

Can an app tell me what a text means?

An app can suggest interpretations. Honne lets you paste a conversation and get AI-generated interpretations and possible replies.

Is the interpretation accurate?

Treat it as a suggestion, not a fact about someone's feelings. When in doubt, ask the person directly.

Privacy

Conversations you paste are sent to an AI service to generate suggestions. Remove names or details you do not want to share.
