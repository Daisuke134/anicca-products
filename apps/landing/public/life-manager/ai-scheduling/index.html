Life Manager — AI Scheduling

Your day, planned and re-planned by an agent that never sleeps

executive-level assistance for your whole life and business

Life Manager's AI scheduling takes your goals, messages, deadlines, and recurring routines, and turns them into a working plan for the day. It prioritizes what matters, protects time for the work only you can do, and flags what will not fit — before it becomes a missed deadline.

The plan is not a suggestion you still have to execute. Life Manager is an agent: it carries out the scheduled work across your connected accounts and reports back what got done. Working 24/7, exclusively for you.

Automatically re-plans your day whenever something comes up

Plans change. A client replies, a booking lands, a deadline moves. Life Manager recalculates the rest of the day when that happens, so you stay on track without an hour of manual reorganization.

  • When a new message, booking, or deadline arrives, the remaining plan is reshuffled around it — today's work and the rest of the week.
  • Work is scheduled against your actual capacity. When more is planned than a day can hold, Life Manager flags the overload early, so expectations can be reset before something is dropped.
  • The whole day is visible in one plan — messages, tasks, deadlines, and routines — so bottlenecks are spotted early and nothing catches you off guard.

Perfectly ordered scheduling. Zero manual effort.

  • Each day is planned from deadlines, duration, and priority. No more missed work and last-minute fire drills.

How AI scheduling works in Life Manager

  1. Connect the accounts your life and business run on — email, calendar, messaging, and the platforms your work lives on.
  2. State your goals and routines. What must happen daily, weekly, and by which deadlines. Priorities are yours; the planning is not.
  3. The agent builds today's schedule — and executes it: replies sent, posts published, applications submitted, reports written, at the times the plan sets.
  4. When something new arrives, the plan is rebuilt and the change is reported. You read one summary instead of re-planning the day yourself.

FAQ

What is AI scheduling in Life Manager?

It is how Life Manager turns goals, messages, deadlines, and recurring routines into a concrete plan for the day. The agent decides what happens when, executes the scheduled work itself, and rebuilds the plan when something new arrives.

How is this different from a calendar app or a to-do list?

A calendar shows events and a to-do list shows tasks; neither does the work. Life Manager is an agent: it plans the day, carries out the scheduled work across your connected accounts, and reports the results. The schedule is a means, not the product.

What happens when my plans change mid-day?

The plan is rebuilt. New messages, bookings, and deadlines are folded into the remaining day, lower-priority work moves, and recurring routines keep their slots. You see what changed in the report instead of re-planning by hand.

Is my data used to train anything?

Life Manager Local runs on your own computer, under your own accounts. The product exists to operate your life and business — not to collect it.
