Life Manager — Habit Tracking

Routines that survive your busiest week

executive-level assistance for your whole life and business

Life Manager's habit tracking is built for the routines your results actually depend on: the daily report, the daily post, the weekly review, the inbox sweep. Each routine is placed in your plan automatically at its rhythm — and kept there by an agent that works 24/7, not by your spare attention.

A streak app asks you to log what you did. Life Manager does the executable parts itself and reports every repetition — kept, moved, or missed, with evidence. Working 24/7, exclusively for you.

Get Life Manager

Life Manager Local is open source (MIT) and free to run on your own computer. Life Manager Cloud is the same product, hosted — no machine of yours needs to stay awake.

Two kinds of routines, handled two ways

  • Routines an agent can run — daily reports, inbox sweeps, scheduled posts, weekly reviews, recurring applications. Life Manager executes these itself, at the set time, and the report links the output.
  • Routines only you can do — exercise, study, practice, the call only you can make. Life Manager protects the slot, prepares what can be prepared around it, and records whether it happened.
  • Either way, the record is the report — not a checkbox you tick from memory at midnight.

Kept, moved, or missed — never silently dropped

  • Routines keep their rhythm when the day is re-planned: daily stays daily, weekly stays weekly, and the time slot holds unless the day truly cannot hold it.
  • A repetition that moves is reported as moved, with its new time. A repetition that is missed is reported as missed, with the reason.
  • Because the agent runs around the clock, a routine scheduled for early morning or late night runs then — not when you happen to wake your laptop.
  • Over weeks, the report shows the pattern: which routines hold, which keep slipping, and what they collide with — the evidence you need to fix the routine instead of blaming yourself.

How habit tracking works in Life Manager

  1. Connect the accounts your routines run on — email, calendar, messaging, and the platforms your work lives on.
  2. State your routines and their rhythm. What must happen daily, weekly, and at what times. The rhythm is yours; the keeping is not.
  3. The agent places and runs them — executing the routines it can execute, protecting the slots of the ones only you can do.
  4. You read the report — kept, moved, missed — and adjust the routine, not your willpower.

  • Creators and sellers whose publishing rhythm is their distribution

FAQ

What is habit tracking in Life Manager?

It is how recurring routines are kept: each routine is placed automatically at its rhythm, the agent executes the routines it can execute itself, protects the slot for the ones only you can do, and reports every repetition as kept, moved, or missed — with evidence.

How is this different from a habit tracker app?

A habit tracker records what you did and shows you a streak; the keeping is still on you. Life Manager schedules the routine, does the executable parts itself, and its report is the record. The schedule is a means, not the product.

What happens when a routine collides with something urgent?

Routines keep their slots by default. When the day is rebuilt, a routine moves only when it truly cannot fit — and the move, or the miss, is reported with the reason. Nothing silently disappears.

Is my data used to train anything?

Life Manager Local runs on your own computer, under your own accounts. The product exists to operate your life and business — not to collect it.
