Life Manager — Task Management

Tasks that get done — by the agent, not by your willpower

executive-level assistance for your whole life and business

Life Manager's task management takes everything on your plate — to-dos, messages, deadlines, recurring routines — and turns it into scheduled work. The agent prioritizes it, places it in your day against your real capacity, and flags what will not fit before it becomes a missed deadline.

The list is not homework you still have to do. Life Manager is an agent: it carries out the scheduled work across your connected accounts and reports back what got done, with the result attached. Working 24/7, exclusively for you.

Get Life Manager

Life Manager Local is open source (MIT) and free to run on your own computer. Life Manager Cloud is the same product, hosted — no machine of yours needs to stay awake.

A task is done when its result exists

Most task systems record intentions. Life Manager records outcomes:

  • A reply is done when it has been sent — not when it sits in a list marked "reply to client".
  • An application is done when it has been submitted, with the posting it answered linked in the report.
  • A scheduled post is done when it is published, at the time the plan set — and the report carries the live link, not a screenshot of a queue.
  • Work that cannot be completed is reported as blocked, with the reason — never silently dropped, never quietly marked done.

Prioritized against your capacity, not your optimism

  • Each task is planned from deadline, duration, and priority. Your highest-priority work keeps its place even when the day changes.
  • When more arrives than a day can hold, the overload is flagged early, lower-priority work moves, and recurring routines keep their slots.
  • At any moment there is one clear next action. The agent always knows what the #1 priority is — and so do you, from the report.
  • New messages, bookings, and deadlines are folded into the remaining day automatically; the plan is rebuilt instead of rotting.

How task management works in Life Manager

  1. Connect the accounts your life and business run on — email, calendar, messaging, and the platforms your work lives on.
  2. Everything that arrives becomes a task with a deadline and a priority. Priorities are yours; the planning and the doing are not.
  3. The agent schedules and executes — replies sent, posts published, applications submitted, reports written, at the times the plan sets.
  4. You read one report of what was completed, what moved, and what is blocked — instead of auditing five apps to find out.

FAQ

What is task management in Life Manager?

It is the loop from capture to completion: everything that lands in your inboxes and routines becomes a task with a deadline and a priority, the agent schedules it, executes the work itself where it can, and reports the result. A task is only "done" when its outcome exists.

How is this different from a to-do list app?

A to-do list stores tasks and waits for you. Life Manager is an agent: it decides when each task happens, does the scheduled work across your connected accounts, and checks it off with a result attached. The schedule is a means, not the product.

What happens when more tasks arrive than fit in a day?

Work is scheduled against your actual capacity. The overload is flagged early, lower-priority work moves, and the deadlines that matter are protected — you see what changed in the report instead of discovering it at midnight.

Is my data used to train anything?

Life Manager Local runs on your own computer, under your own accounts. The product exists to operate your life and business — not to collect it.
