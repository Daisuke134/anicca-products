Life Manager — Focus Time

Protected time for the work only you can do

executive-level assistance for your whole life and business

Life Manager's focus time reserves blocks for your highest-priority work — placed against your deadlines and your real capacity — and defends them when the day changes. Lower-priority work moves. The block holds.

And unlike a blocked calendar slot, the work around the block does not pile up while you focus. Life Manager is an agent: it executes the surrounding work itself — replies, posts, applications, reports — and shows you the results when you surface. Working 24/7, exclusively for you.

Get Life Manager

Life Manager Local is open source (MIT) and free to run on your own computer. Life Manager Cloud is the same product, hosted — no machine of yours needs to stay awake.

Defended, not just reserved

  • Focus blocks are placed from deadlines, duration, and priority — sized to the work, at the time of day the work deserves.
  • When a new message, booking, or deadline arrives, the plan is rebuilt around the block. Minor interruptions never reach your most important deadlines.
  • Work is scheduled against your actual capacity. When more is planned than a day can hold, the overload is flagged early — focus time is protected by shrinking the plan, not the block.
  • If a block ever has to move, the move is reported with the reason. Nothing is silently overwritten.

Clear around the block, not just quiet inside it

  • The inbox does not wait for you: messages that arrive during the block are handled or queued with drafts, and only what needs your judgment is surfaced afterwards.
  • Recurring routines — daily reports, scheduled posts, weekly reviews — run during the block without touching it.
  • When the block ends, one report covers what the agent did, what changed, and what is next — so re-entry takes minutes, not an hour of archaeology.

How focus time works in Life Manager

  1. Connect the accounts your life and business run on — email, calendar, messaging, and the platforms your work lives on.
  2. Mark the work only you can do and how much uninterrupted time it needs. Priorities are yours; the defense is not.
  3. The agent builds the day around the blocks — and executes everything else at the times the plan sets.
  4. When something new arrives, the plan is rebuilt around the blocks, and the change is reported.

FAQ

What is focus time in Life Manager?

It is a protected block reserved for the work only you can do. The agent places it against your deadlines and capacity, defends it when the day is re-planned, and clears the work around it by executing that work itself.

How is this different from calendar blocking or a focus app?

Calendar blocking reserves a slot and hopes; a focus app silences notifications while work piles up. Life Manager defends the block when plans change, and the surrounding work does not pile up — the agent does it. The schedule is a means, not the product.

What happens to my focus block when something urgent arrives?

Your highest-priority work keeps its place. New work is folded into the remaining day and lower-priority work moves first. A focus block moves only when it truly cannot fit — and the change is reported.

Is my data used to train anything?

Life Manager Local runs on your own computer, under your own accounts. The product exists to operate your life and business — not to collect it.
