FAQ

What is the difference between Anicca and I am?

Scale and stance. I am is the established default with 10M+ installs; Anicca is the smaller, newer app whose maker publishes everything. Both deliver daily affirmations; they differ in library size, track record, and how much of the business you can inspect.

Is Anicca bigger than I am?

No. I am's estimated monthly app revenue (~$702K) is roughly four orders of magnitude above Anicca's entire company revenue so far. Anicca competes on focus and transparency while it grows.[2][3]

Which one should I choose?

Choose I am for the largest library and the proven track record. Choose Anicca if a focused app, a Lifetime option, and an auditable maker matter to you. Both are free to download — a week with each settles it better than any comparison page, including this one.
