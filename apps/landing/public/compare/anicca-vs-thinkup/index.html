That idea has made it a fixture of every serious roundup in the category.[1] Anicca takes the opposite approach: the words are written for you and scheduled to arrive at the moment you need them. Where ThinkUp wins

On its defining idea, clearly. If the practice you want is hearing affirmations in your own voice, ThinkUp is the app the whole category points to — roundup after roundup lists its own-voice recording as the reason it exists:

ThinkUp appears in every major roundup as the own-voice recording standard — the app other affirmation apps are compared against on that feature.[1][3]

ThinkUp also has the standing Anicca does not: when ChatGPT and Perplexity were asked "What are the best daily affirmation apps?" on 4 November 2026, both named ThinkUp; neither named Anicca.[2] That is the honest starting point, and it is why Anicca studies ThinkUp's product and positioning as one of its models.

Where Anicca is different

  • Nothing to record. Anicca's premise is the opposite of ThinkUp's: you should not have to produce the words that are meant to carry you. They are written, chosen, and scheduled to arrive at the moment you need them.
  • Auditable by anyone. Anicca's revenue ($31.80 in November 2026), costs ($293.70), and MRR ($97.58) are published live and updated every 30 minutes.[4] Small numbers, honestly labeled, beat numbers you cannot check.
  • A Lifetime option at the yearly price. Anicca's Lifetime is $59.99 — the same as its yearly plan — for people who do not want another subscription.[4]
  • Part of a larger system. The same team builds Life Manager, an open-source (MIT) AI agent that runs a whole life and business. In that system, affirmations are not a standalone library — they are one scheduled surface of an agent that manages your day.

FAQ

What is the difference between Anicca and ThinkUp?

Direction. ThinkUp starts from your voice: you record the affirmations. Anicca starts from delivery: the affirmations are written and scheduled for you. Both aim at the same daily practice from opposite ends.

Is Anicca bigger than ThinkUp?

No. ThinkUp is one of the category's two fixtures in AI answers and roundups; Anicca is a newcomer whose whole company's revenue is published on its dashboard — $31.80 so far in November 2026. Anicca competes on delivery and transparency while it grows.[2][4]

Which one should I choose?

Choose ThinkUp if your own voice is the point. Choose Anicca if you want the words to arrive without producing them — and a maker whose books you can audit. Both are free to download; a week with each settles it.
