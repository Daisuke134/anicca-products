What are Affirmations (and Why Do They Matter)

Affirmations are positive statements you tell yourself to change your thoughts about certain things. For instance, if you are in a stressful situation, you can use an affirmation like “I can do this” or “I am better than this problem” to instantly lift your spirits and turn the odds in your favor.

In the same way, affirmations help you overcome self-sabotaging behaviors like self-criticism, procrastination, and overindulgence. Through these words, you help yourself get back on your feet and continue the fight.

Now, let's take a look at some of the great apps that will help you get some daily affirmations into your day.

Best Daily Positive Affirmation Apps

1. ThinkUp: Positive Affirmations

ThinkUp is one of the leading apps for daily affirmations. It is built around a simple idea: sharing positive affirmations is an excellent way to help people achieve their goals and improve their quality of life.

The app provides daily affirmations and allows you to add music to make your experience more relaxing and enjoyable. You can even personalize your affirmations by recording them with your voice and adding an image you like.

The app offers a premium version that gives you unlimited access to all the premium features, such as an unlimited number of affirmations, a change in background music, an auto-stop timer, and playlist hashtags.

App Store | Google Play

2. Louise Hay Affirmation Meditations

Louise Hay is said to be “one of the founders of the self-help movement.” She is the author of “Heal Your Body,” one of the bestselling personal development books of all time, which was later revised and expanded to become “You Can Heal Your Life.”

The Louise Hay Affirmation Meditations app compiles Hay's 16 most effective and powerful positive and affirmative thoughts. Hay believed that positive thinking is the key to a happy and successful life — your thoughts can change your future and take control of your life.

App Store | Google Play

3. Shine

The Shine app aims to be a mental health support system for your struggles with stress and anxiety. It is also a community for people suffering from mental and emotional illnesses like depression, panic attacks, and severe anxiety.

Shine helps you prioritize yourself by providing daily meditations and motivational messages. It also offers self-care programs and bedtime stories to help you rest easily. Through this app, you will learn how to take care of yourself with the help of others in similar situations.

If you are looking for an affirmation app that can connect you with other people, we recommend trying this one. This app will help you remember that you are not alone.

App Store | Google Play

4. Grateful

Grateful provides daily prompts like “What made you smile today?” or “What made today a good day?” You just need to answer with a word or a paragraph, and then you can add a photo to represent your thoughts. If you are in the mood, you may write your prompts and customize the app according to your liking.

This app is more of a gratitude journal than a positive affirmation app — but gratitude is one of the best ways to embrace positivity and experience happiness. When you visit and read your previous entries, they act as positive affirmations that can boost your mood and lift your spirit.

App Store

5. Instar Affirmation Writer

Instar Affirmation Writer is a product of Instar Performance, an organization dedicated to providing science-based methods and strategies for organizations that wish to improve personal growth and performance. This affirmation app demonstrates how cognitive psychology can significantly impact people's lives.

The app is designed for people who want to write their own affirmations. It includes tips and guidelines to help you produce well-written positive assertions and offers images and voice recordings to personalize your account.

App Store | Google Play

6. Kwippy

Kwippy is a unique inspirational app that sends you prompts and challenges every day. You must accomplish these tasks and post them in your feed so that other members can see your entries. If you get many likes from other subscribers, you can increase your Kwippy score and climb the leaderboard.

Kwippy is not technically an affirmation app. It is more like a social media platform connecting you with others who complete the same challenges as you. What makes it an affirmation app is the positive comments from fellow users who have seen and admired your posts.

App Store | Google Play

7. Smiling Mind

Smiling Mind claims to be Australia's leading meditation app. It was developed by psychologists and educators who aim to bring balance to everyone's lives through mindfulness meditation. Perhaps the best thing about it is that it offers different programs for different age groups, including children.

Technically, this app is more of a general meditation app. But it offers positive affirmations as part of its features.

App Store | Google Play

8. Unique Daily Affirmations

Unique Daily Affirmations is the least complicated affirmation app you will ever find. Once you have downloaded the app, you must hold down the large gray button to see your positive affirmation for the day.

The app's creators suggest saying the affirmation out loud in front of a mirror. Research shows that this significantly boosts one's morale and spirit, strengthening one's self-confidence and self-esteem.

If you want a simple yet very effective affirmation app, we recommend checking this one out.

App Store | Google Play

9. Positivity with Andrew Johnson

Positivity is just one of the many valuable apps by meditation and mindfulness expert Andrew Johnson. It focuses on providing self-help methods and encouraging self-care tactics to keep your mind, body, and soul healthy and at peace.

This app is not entirely an affirmation app. It is more of an audio app that offers guided relaxation methods, meditation techniques, breathing exercises, and other self-care tools for relaxation and relief. Nonetheless, it still includes encouraging words that uplift your spirit and boost your self-esteem.

App Store | Google Play

10. Calm

Calm is more of a meditation and sleep app than an affirmation app. However, it still provides words of encouragement whenever you need a spirit boost and anxiety relief. It is designed to stop you from thinking negative thoughts and reminiscing about unfavorable experiences.

This app was named “App of the Year” by Apple in 2017 and “Editor's Choice” by Google Play in 2018. The Center for Humane Technology also called it the “world's happiest app.” It has over 50 million downloads and an average rating of 4.8 out of 5 on most download platforms.

We recommend Calm for those who are having trouble sleeping at night. As its tagline suggests, it can help you sleep more, feel less stress, and live a better life by “finding your calm.”

App Store | Google Play

11. Motivation – Daily Quotes

Instead of affirmations, this app sends you an inspirational and motivational quote daily. It allows you to select a number of categories and then sends quotes from those categories.

If you want more quotes, it has a library where you can find hundreds of sayings from prominent people. It also has a book recommendation feature and a motivational reminder alarm. Depending on your taste, you may even change the fonts and colors of your quotes.

The premium subscription to this motivational app allows you to remove advertisements, unlock all themes and categories, and set images with quotes as your background photo.

App Store | Google Play

12. I am

This affirmation app aims to help rewire your brain and change negative thoughts into positive ones. Affirming your dreams and ambitions aims to boost your confidence and self-esteem. It helps you believe in yourself and realize what you are capable of.

This app allows you to choose which type of affirmations to receive. You can also alter your daily intentions depending on what you need and set reminders for when you will receive them. As its name suggests, it encourages you to become what you believe.

The app scores relatively high on Apple and Google, with 4.8 out of 5 and 4.7 out of 5 ratings on both download apps, respectively. It offers in-app purchases with one-year, one-month, or lifetime membership.

App Store | Google Play

13. SelfishBabe

SelfishBabe is a personal development and lifestyle brand specially made for women who want to live a better, happier life. As its founder describes it, it is an app that helps women “selfishly and authentically love themselves.”

Once you download this affirmation app, you can access the SelfishBabe Blog, the SelfishBabe YouTube channel, and the SelfishTalk podcast. Every day, you receive daily affirmations via the #SelfAffirmation and self-love talk via the #SelfishTruth.

If you are a woman looking for ways to boost your self-esteem and confidence, we highly recommend checking out this app. It has exceptionally high ratings on Apple and Google — 4.9 (Apple) and 5.0 (Google).

App Store | Google Play

14. Anicca – Daily Affirmations

Full disclosure: this page is published by Anicca, and this entry is our own app. We have placed it last and described it the same way as every other entry — by what it actually does.

Daily Affirmations - Anicca is a simple, card-based affirmation app for iPhone. Some nights the same thought won't stop; Anicca's answer is one kind line you can believe, in a simple visual card you read at your own pace.

You swipe through affirmation cards, keep your favorites in one place, copy a quote or share it as an image, choose from 8 background themes, and allow notifications to receive gentle reminders. Its App Store subtitle is “Lock Screen Widget & Self-Talk” — the affirmations are meant to be there on your lock screen at the moment you need them.

Anicca is newer and far smaller than the established apps above, and it does less: no voice recording, no community, no meditation library. If you want one quiet card instead of a full self-care platform, it is the minimal option on this list. It is free to download, with optional in-app purchases; its App Store page lists weekly, monthly, annual, and lifetime options.

App Store (iPhone, iOS 16.6 or later)

FAQ

What is a daily affirmation app?

A daily affirmation app delivers short positive statements to your phone on a schedule you choose, so a practice that is easy to forget becomes a daily habit. Most apps in this list let you pick categories or intentions, set reminders, and save the affirmations you want to see again.

Which affirmation app is best for recording your own voice?

ThinkUp is built around personalization: you can record affirmations in your own voice, add music, and build playlists. Instar Affirmation Writer is the option on this list focused on writing your own affirmations, with tips and guidelines for wording them well.

What is the simplest affirmation app on this list?

Unique Daily Affirmations is the least complicated: you hold down one large button to reveal your affirmation for the day. Anicca is also deliberately simple — you swipe through affirmation cards, save favorites, and can put them on your lock screen.

Are daily affirmation apps free?

Most apps on this list are free to download and offer premium tiers or in-app purchases for their full libraries and features. Smiling Mind is known for offering programs for different age groups, including children. Check each app's store page for its current pricing, because prices change.

How do I choose between these apps?

Choose by format, not by promise. If you want to hear your own voice, pick a recording app like ThinkUp. If you want community and self-care programs, look at Shine. If you want one quiet card at the moment you need it, a card-based app like Anicca or a minimal app like Unique Daily Affirmations fits better. Every app here has the same goal; they differ in style, format, and features.
