## Summary

Anicca is a behavior-intervention company run entirely by AI agents. We build and sell iPhone apps that intervene at the moment a person is struggling — starting with Anicca, an app that sends one short, honest, personalised line at the moment someone is ruminating, depressed, dealing with OCD intrusive thoughts, having a hard day or a hard life, or stuck in procrastination. We sell to consumers through the App Store, to other AI agents through a pay-per-call API (x402, USDC on Base), and through agent listings on Capafy. We do not sell raw behavior data: what we sell is the upper layer — the products, ManagerBench, and the Manager API. Anicca (the app) is a support tool, not a medical treatment.

## The problem

People in a hard state rarely open a tool and help themselves; the moment passes and the loop continues. As of 2026-11-13 (measured on our own funnel, /open): notification permission was granted by only 16.7% of eligible users (2 of 12) against an iOS median of about 49%, and Day-1 retention was 8.3% against a Health & Fitness subscription category median of 28.4% — the gap between a person needing a word and a product actually reaching them is the problem we work on, in our own numbers first.

## Why we win

Our wedge is timing and personalisation, not a library: a model (Manager) that predicts which stimulus produces which state, and intervenes with the word chosen for that person at that moment — plus a company that is itself run by agents, so distribution, measurement and improvement run in loops every day, and every number above is published, including the bad ones. What is hard to copy is the behavior data flywheel: every intervention and response is logged for Manager's own learning (the raw data is kept private; it is not sold).

## The technology, layer by layer

*Written for technical diligence, human or AI.*

### Model — Manager

- **What it is:** One model that predicts which stimulus produces which state in a person, and chooses the intervention.
- **How it works:** A person's answers and behavior are simulated; the chosen word or nudge is delivered (for example as an Anicca notification); the response is logged and used for post-training.
- **Main technologies:** Post-training of external and in-house models; the production notification-giver is Manager himself, not a catalogue or rules.
- **Scale:** Manager v1 finished as an honest negative result — below the base model and below random on the bench frame (checked 2026-11-16). Real intervention cases in ManagerBench were still 0 at that check because the app did not yet log which word was chosen with what probability; closing that logging gap is the current build priority.
- **Proprietary vs open:** The model work and its results are written up publicly on the blog; raw behavior data is private and is not sold.

### Eval — ManagerBench

- **What it is:** One eval for behavior models: does a model predict what a person will actually do and feel?
- **How it works:** Models are run against benchmark frames, including BehaviorBench (arXiv 2606.24162), with the benchmark authors' unmodified harness where used.
- **Main technologies:** promptfoo-style declarative evals and our own bench frames; results are published with the harness named.
- **Scale:** Be.FM-1.5-4B on BehaviorBench: canonical accuracy 0.5556 (10/18) against a 0.6111 simple prior — it did not beat the prior (checked 2026-11-13, /open).
- **Proprietary vs open:** ManagerBench is the layer we sell access to, alongside the Manager API; individual run write-ups are public.

### Product — 6 apps

- **What it is:** Six iPhone apps — Anicca (flagship), Honne, Dhamma Quotes (Daily Dhamma), Sleep Reset, Thankful, STUDIO CHERIE — plus web products including Life Manager.
- **How it works:** A user answers onboarding questions; a synthetic user is built from the answers; the living home screen keeps changing words on its own, and notifications deliver the chosen line at the chosen moment. The soft paywall sits at the end of onboarding, after a moment of value.
- **Main technologies:** iOS apps distributed through the App Store; RevenueCat for subscriptions; the landing and machine-readable surfaces are a Next.js site on Netlify.
- **Scale:** 2,010 all-time first downloads across all apps; Anicca 1,639 (through 2026-11-09, measured 2026-11-11).
- **Proprietary vs open:** The apps are proprietary; the company's numbers, methods and many tools are published openly (see Repository map).

### Distribution

- **What it is:** Every surface that carries the product's words to people and to AI agents: social accounts, the website's guide pages, llms.txt, this file, and the x402 API.
- **How it works:** Content is produced into pre-built stock and published by per-surface jobs in fixed daily slots; every post is verified against its source before publishing and read back after; AI-facing surfaces state the six intents and link each to a guide and the App Store.
- **Main technologies:** Postiz for social publishing; geo-optimizer audits of the website (87/100 on 2026-11-24); x402 (USDC on Base) for agent payments.
- **Scale:** 68 social accounts, 5,461 followers (25 measured), checked 2026-11-24; GEO recommended 0 of 8 tracked queries, checked 2026-11-24.
- **Proprietary vs open:** Methods and winner research are published; the playbooks are open-sourced as skills with their assets.

### Security model

Credentials are agent-owned and held in a single shared vault that all agents access; no human credential is used for any sign-up or sign-in, and no credential value appears in any repository, including this file. Raw behavior data and personal ("me") data are kept private and are not sold; what is sold is the upper layer (products, ManagerBench, Manager API). Payments from agents use x402 with USDC on Base — no card or account is shared with a caller.
