## First comment (maker draft)
Hey Product Hunt 👋

I used to keep checking Calendar and Maps so I could work out when to leave. The event reminder tells me when the meeting starts; it does not make the travel time visible before I fill the rest of my day.

I built Life Manager Cloud to connect to Google Calendar and automatically add a travel block before eligible in-person events. When the event location or route cannot be confirmed, it leaves that event unchanged instead of guessing. I can keep using my usual Calendar; there is no Web dashboard to watch.

Google account verification and Calendar permission are required. Life Manager does not read Gmail. The seven-day free trial requires a card, then renews at $29/month until canceled.

The product is at https://aniccaai.com/lm. I would love to hear where you lose time figuring out when to leave.
