# Anicca — Winner Map

Who Anicca copies, in every space. One winner per account, fixed by name.
Published 2026-11-05 as part of Anicca's open operating playbook.

## The rule: Copy Winner, Do More

We win by copying the winner in each space and doing more quantity than the
winner. The goal is not to reach the winner's level — it is to override the
winner. This applies to platform businesses exactly as it applies to social
distribution: sign up, log in, follow the winner of that space, and out-do
their quantity in registrations, applications, listings, and posts.

How a winner is chosen (in this order):

1. Same niche of the niche — literally the same niche and the same product
   form (an affirmation app copies an account that wins by selling an
   affirmation app).
2. Numerically winning in that space.
3. Fully reproducible — the form must work without a specific person's face
   or acting. Face-led forms are disqualified however large the audience.

Each platform copies only that platform's winner and that winner's own
winning post. Moving a winner's post across platforms does not count as a
copy on the new platform.

Class A = a winner selling a competing product. Class B (same niche, the
audience is the buyer, reproducible form) is used only where no Class A
winner exists, and is always labeled Class B.
