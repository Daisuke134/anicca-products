# Anicca — Playbook: Copy the Winner

How Anicca grows every account, product, and platform business.
Published 2026-11-05 as part of Anicca's open operating playbook.
Companion files: CULTURE.md (values), WINNERS.md (who we copy),
POSTING-SCHEDULE.md (when we post).

## The formula

Copy Winner, Do More. Copy the winner in each space, in full, and do more
quantity than the winner. The goal is not to reach the winner's level —
it is to override the winner. This applies to social distribution and to
platform businesses (sign up, log in, follow the winner of that space,
out-do their quantity in registrations, applications, listings, posts).

Nothing original is invented first. Originality is what an untrained
default produces; winning methods are post-training: explore outside,
open the winner's real work, copy it, measure, repeat.

## Choosing the winner

In this order:

1. Niche of the niche of the niche. Literally the same niche and the same
   product form. An affirmation app copies an account that wins by selling
   an affirmation app — the app company's own account first.
2. Numerically winning inside that niche.
3. Fully reproducible. The form must work without a specific person's
   face, acting, or personal character. A face-led form is disqualified
   however large its audience is.

Class A = a winner selling a competing product. Class B (same niche, the
audience matches the buyer, reproducible form) may be fixed only where no
Class A winner exists, and is labeled Class B. Never mix the two.

One account fixes exactly one main winner. Learning may come from two or
three winners; the executed copy is always pinned to one specific winner
and that winner's one specific winning post. Blended "formats" and
"types" are not copies.

## What gets copied

Everything, end to end: the account itself (name, profile image, bio,
link, pinned posts, highlights), the product (the product itself,
onboarding, paywall, pricing, design), and the distribution (post form,
hook, caption, music, cadence, posting times). Copying is per platform:
each platform copies that platform's winner and that winner's own post.
Moving a winner's post from one platform to another does not count as a
copy on the new platform.

The source is the winner's successful post specifically — a winner also
has failed posts, and copying a failed post produces a failure.

## Before anything is published

1. Open the winner's winning post itself. No link is ever reported as a
   copy source until the reporter has opened it and confirmed (a) the
   form is reproducible without a specific person's face or acting, and
   (b) our post's form actually matches it.
2. Put the winner's post and our output side by side and count the
   differences: first frame, hook, subtitle style and timing, cuts,
   length, audio and music, caption, call to action, form.
3. If any difference remains, it is not published. Zero differences is
   the pass line — color, text, and sound included.
4. Audio gate: a video ships only if it has an audio track whose content
   matches the winner's form (narration and/or music) at real volume.
   Silent videos never ship.

## Cadence

Posting frequency and posting times are copied from the winner's measured
cadence. Where a winner's times are not yet measured, the slots are filled
with published best practice — unmeasured winner times never stop
posting. The fixed slots live in POSTING-SCHEDULE.md. A post counts only
when it has a public URL, and every report pairs our post's URL with the
winner original's URL.

## Measure, then iterate

Every post is measured (views, likes, comments, shares, saves, and the
revenue behind it), and our numbers are placed next to the winner's
numbers. Flat metrics — zero likes, no follower growth, no improvement —
are treated as an emergency: fix by hand right now, by finding how
winners in the same state fixed it and copying that, and only then put
the fix on a loop. Distribution has two layers and both are worked:
outside distribution (social, articles, search) and inside-platform
distribution (the platform's own search, titles, keywords, categories,
and ranking), measured by in-platform views and purchases.

Copying never completes. Each round leaves a list of remaining
differences, and the next round closes them. Quantity, quality, and copy
depth must each be above yesterday, every day.

## Platform businesses

Enter every place where money can be made, provided the full path —
registration, listing, sale, payout — completes with no human in the
loop. Before entering, read not only the winners' profiles and listings
but also the advice the winners themselves have written (their own posts
and articles about what works): the details that observation alone misses
are written there. Then copy all of it — market, profile, visuals,
proposal text, pricing, operations — and run more volume than anyone
else in that space.
