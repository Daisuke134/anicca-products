# Anicca — Playbook: Free English Distribution

Distribution for Anicca's apps and agent products is free, organic,
and English-first: the English market is the larger one, and paid
distribution is a structure error — distribution itself earns
nothing; the product behind it does. These are the standing rules
for working English-language surfaces at zero spend.

## Order of operations

1. Existing products before new ones.
2. English surfaces before Japanese ones.
3. Zero spend. A tactic that needs a budget is a different tactic.
4. No human in the loop: registration, posting, and measurement all
   complete agent-side.

## Rank by expected installs per unit of effort

Lanes are worked in ranked order, and the ranking is re-measured,
never assumed. The families that consistently rank highest:

- Dedicated niche communities and forums where self-promotion is
  allowed under stated rules, answered in the community's own style.
- Free launch and directory boards for apps and tools.
- App-store optimisation (below) — the only lane that compounds
  while we sleep.
- Search pages: one page per user intent, written to be cited.
- Image boards with vertical, save-worthy assets.
- Newsletter and notes surfaces where builders already read.

## Check the platform before touching it

For every candidate surface, record before the first post:

- Can an account be created with email alone?
- What are the written self-promotion rules?
- What does the surface's own winner post, how often, and in what
  format?
- The verdict: work it, watch it, or skip it — with the reason.

A surface whose rules forbid the work is skipped, not gamed.

## Pacing and warm-up

- New accounts start at half volume for the first week.
- Genuine replies and participation come before promotional posts.
- Per-account daily caps follow the platform's own published limits,
  with spacing between posts; volume is raised only on measured
  response, never on hope.
- Every account still copies exactly one fixed winner for its form
  and cadence (see WINNERS.md and POSTING-SCHEDULE.md).

## Templates and measurement

- Posts are written from a numbered template bank; each template
  keeps the source it was copied from.
- Every link carries a campaign tag per template and per channel,
  so installs are attributable to the exact post type that earned
  them.
- Weekly, templates are ranked by installs per post. The bottom is
  killed; the top is doubled. Views never make this decision —
  conversion does.

## ASO, in brief

- Localisation multiplies keyword slots; each locale is its own
  surface with its own winner.
- No word is repeated across name, subtitle, and keyword field.
- Low-competition keywords with real search intent beat head terms.
- The first screenshot sells the outcome, not the interface.
- Review prompts fire after a success moment, never on a timer.
- Paid search ads are not used under the zero-spend rule.

## The scout loop

This playbook is a living file. Daily scouting on X and the web
adds tactics — each with its source post, labelled confirmed or
self-reported — and removes what stopped working. A tactic with no
source and no measurement is deleted, not debated.
